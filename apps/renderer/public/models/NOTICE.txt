selfie_segmenter.tflite
=======================

MediaPipe SelfieSegmenter (square, 256x256 input, float16), used by LiveScape
for local subject segmentation.

  Source:  https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/
           selfie_segmenter/float16/latest/selfie_segmenter.tflite
  Authors: Tingbo Hou, Siargey Pisarchyk, Karthik Raveendran (Google)
  Dated:   6 May 2021
  License: Apache License, Version 2.0
           https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The model card records the intended application as human segmentation from
video in interactive applications, and states that the model is optimized for
real-time performance rather than pixel-perfect masks. Surveillance and
identity recognition are named as out of scope, and LiveScape does neither.

The MediaPipe Tasks Vision runtime that executes this model is also licensed
under Apache 2.0, and is published from node_modules at build time rather than
committed. See docs/camera.md.
